The first paragraph is the lead. It is set larger than the rest, so use it to say what the piece is about in two or three sentences.

## Each slide becomes a section

Write two or three sentences under each heading. Headings that start with two hashes also appear in the “In this piece” list on the left. You can use **bold**, *italics* and [links](/work/luxury-launches).

> A line that starts with a greater-than sign becomes a pull quote.

## Show the original carousel

Put the slides between the two marker lines and they become a strip the reader can swipe.
